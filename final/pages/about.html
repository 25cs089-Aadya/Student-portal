About StudentHub

StudentHub is an all-in-one portal designed to centralize student information, academic logs, and campus administrative processes.

By combining timetables, attendance registers, financial data, and exam results under a unified responsive dashboard, StudentHub removes the complexity of navigating disconnected systems, making everyday campus life simpler.

What We Provide

Features designed to support your daily academic journey

📚

Academic Information

Get real-time updates on class timetables, attendance ratios, and grade cards instantly.

🔔

Campus Updates

Never miss a beat. Check upcoming events, workshops, fee deadlines, and circulars.

💳

Student Services

Track pending dues, review past semester billing, and get in touch with administration departments.
